03

Research

Two completed bachelor's theses on CXCR4: molecule design and cancer bioinformatics.

THESIS-01İnönü University · Supervisor: Dr. Seçil Demiral

Structure-Based Design and Computational Evaluation of Amentoflavone Derivatives as Novel CXCR4 Antagonists

  • —I designed five amentoflavone derivatives as candidate CXCR4 antagonists, using the crystal structure 3ODU.
  • —I docked the compounds with AutoDock Vina. Under the same protocol, Fluoro-Deoxy-Amentoflavone scored −11.9 kcal/mol and Plerixafor scored −9.4 kcal/mol. I prioritized Fluoro-Deoxy for experimental evaluation.
  • —I used SwissADME for ADME and drug-likeness predictions, ProTox-II for toxicity predictions and BIOVIA Discovery Studio for ligand–receptor interaction analysis.
  • —The docking poses suggested better receptor fit after selective deoxygenation, including a predicted hydrogen bond with Tyr55.
Bachelor's ThesisView thesis study
THESIS-02İnönü University · Supervisor: Dr. Samet Kocabay

A Bioinformatic Investigation into the Role of the CXCR4 Chemokine Receptor in Cancer Progression and Metastasis: Expression, Prognosis, and Immune Infiltration

  • —I examined CXCR4 expression, genomic changes, survival associations and immune infiltration in TCGA breast (BRCA), lung (LUAD) and colorectal (COAD/READ) cancer datasets.
  • —I used GEPIA2 for expression analysis, cBioPortal for genomic changes, Kaplan–Meier Plotter for survival, TIMER2.0 for immune infiltration and STRING for protein interaction networks.
  • —In LUAD, higher CXCR4 expression correlated with longer survival (p = 0.007) and greater CD8+ T-cell infiltration. The survival association varied by tissue.
